# Assets and marks

This project is an independent identification catalog. It is not affiliated with or endorsed by any listed institution. Bank names, logos, trademarks, and artwork remain the property of their respective rights holders and are presented here only to identify institutions in software interfaces and catalog search.

The MIT license covers only original project software and original documentation identified by this repository's license notice. It does not grant rights in bank names, logos, trademarks, or third-party artwork. Placement inside an SVG or a raster export does not establish ownership or permission. No asset is marked public domain, CC0, or unrestricted.

## Review status

All supplied exports currently require provenance review. United States identities and current status also remain unverified. India visual pairs were evaluated for completeness and unsafe SVG features, but that evaluation is not a trademark or copyright clearance. SVGs with embedded bitmap data are specifically flagged in the audit reports; the bitmap source and permission status have not been established. Files that cannot be reasonably identified or whose source cannot be established must remain out of any public release.

## Rights-holder contact

For a correction, removal request, or private rights-holder claim, email [banklogos@amitayre.com](mailto:banklogos@amitayre.com). Public issues may also be used for routine factual corrections, but email is the private contact channel.
